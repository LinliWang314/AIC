Why it exists

AI can generate endlessly, but it still does not know what is beautiful, what matters, or what should be kept. AIC exists to bring AI into contact with taste, judgment, and self-expression.

Founder note

AIC began when I realized AI had changed the condition of creativity. Making was no longer the hard part. The harder part was learning how to see, choose, and stay with what mattered. Around the same time, I also began to feel that the boundaries I had once drawn between engineering and art, or between work and life, were less real than I thought. All of them ask for attention. All of them ask for judgment. AIC grew from that recognition, and from a desire to create a space where those connections could be explored more consciously.
