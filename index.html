About

A gathering space for AI, creativity, taste, and self-expression.

We host intimate workshops where people create with AI, reflect on what they make, and share the stories behind their images.

See the gatherings

Why AIC Exists

Judgment in the age of abundance

AI can generate endlessly. What becomes scarce is not output, but the ability to see, choose, and decide what is worth keeping.

A new creative language

Art has always evolved with new tools. AIC explores AI as a medium for images, stories, symbols, and self-expression.

A space for meaning

For many of us, work can become repetitive and detached from personal meaning. AIC creates a space to reflect, create, and ask what matters.

Who It's For

AIC is for curious people across technology, art, design, research, and everyday life who want to use AI as a creative medium: to make images, explore ideas, and think more deeply about identity, imagination, and what matters to them.

From the Founder

Founded in 2025 by Linli Wang, AIC grew from a personal search for meaning across engineering, art, work, and life.

Along the way, I realized that art wasn't simply about creating beautiful things. It changed the way I observe, question, and think. AIC is my way of creating that same experience for others.

Our Home

Gallery House

Menlo Park, CA

Founded in 1958, Gallery House is one of the Bay Area's longest-running artist collectives. Since AIC's second gathering, it has become a home for conversations around AI, art, and human creativity.

We're especially grateful to Peter J. Koltai, Chair of Gallery House, artist, and Professor Emeritus at Stanford University, whose early belief in AIC and continued support have helped shape this community.
